Photograph the tool

Lay the tool flat and take one photo straight from above. The page traces it, scales it to millimetres, and builds a holder that clips onto a Multiboard.

You can also drop an image anywhere on the workspace. Photos are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Best results

  1. Print the calibration mat at 100% scale and lay the tool inside the four black squares.
  2. Shoot from directly above with the whole mat in frame. Use soft, even light and no flash, so shadows stay faint.
  3. Dark or colourful tools on the white mat work best. For chrome or white tools, use a dark cloth with a ruler or card beside the tool, then pick “Reference line” in step 2.

Calibration mat (PDF)

US Letter PDF A4 PDF

Black and white only. Print at “Actual size” (not “Fit to page”), then check the 100 mm bar at the bottom with a ruler.

Set the scale

Tell the page how big things are in the photo. The mat also corrects for a camera that wasn't perfectly overhead.

Drag the four orange crosshairs onto the centres of the black squares if any are off. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

The 100 mm bar didn't print at exactly 100 mm

Measure the distances between marker centres on your print and enter them here. Leave blank to use the nominal values.

Drag the two ends of the line across something of known length in the photo, then enter that length. Put the reference on the same surface as the tool and shoot straight down.

Check the outline

The orange line becomes the pocket. Fix anything the camera got wrong, such as a shadow or a missed edge.

Automatic trace

The AI trace downloads a ~25 MB model the first time (then it’s cached) and works best on busy backgrounds or low-contrast tools. It runs in your browser; the photo isn’t uploaded.

Move left to include faint parts of the tool, right to drop shadows and background.

Edit points

  • Drag a point to move it.
  • Double-click (or double-tap) an edge to add a point.
  • Click a point and press Delete to remove it.

Design the holder

Changes rebuild the 3D model automatically. Everything is in millimetres on the 25 mm Multiboard grid.
